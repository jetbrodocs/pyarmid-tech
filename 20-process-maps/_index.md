# Process Maps

## Main Flow

- [Procurement — Indent to Receipt](proc-01-procurement.md) — Two paths: Path A (HDPE/steel, promoter-run) and Path B (other materials, indent → PO → gap → receipt). Documents the off-system gap between PO and sales order.
- [Fleet Management and LR Tracking](proc-02-fleet-lr.md) — Two separate flows. **Flow A outbound:** own fleet (~100 trucks) and contractors moving finished goods to customers, run by a team of 4 across 9 plants. **Flow B inbound:** third-party carriers bringing raw materials in, tracked by purchase/plant teams, who often collect from the carrier's facility themselves. LR ageing problem (5-8+ days) spans both.
