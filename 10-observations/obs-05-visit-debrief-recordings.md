## 2. IBC Assembly Sequence 🟢 (rec-34)

1. HDPE **blow moulded** into the inner container.
2. **Steel arrives as sheet** — *"the steel comes in a sheet, a ring sheet, which often gets bent and readied for the shape of the cage as well as the shape of the pallet."*
3. Steel cage set up — *"putting on the bolts, putting on the pallet below, putting on the sheet at the bottom that holds the weight of the drum inside."*
4. Valves fitted.
5. Final assembly.

**QA happens at every stage** 🟢, stated four separate ways in rec-34:

> *"Once blow moulded, [it] also gets QA'd. Once put in the valves and everything, it also gets QA'd.
> The steel also gets QA'd. And once it's turned into a cage and the whole IBC container is ready,
> that also is getting QA'd by the team."*

This corroborates the three separate visual-defect standards photographed at Unit 7 — Inner
Container, Grid/Cage & IBC, and Bulk Packaging.

## 5. Inter-Plant Movement — the Tax Rule 🟡 (rec-32)

> *"Direct challan is created for transport from one plant to another **if they are in same GST**.
> But if they are in **different GST, in different state**, then invoice is created for transport."*

| Movement | Document |
|---|---|
| Plant A → Plant B, **same GSTIN** | **Delivery challan** |
| Plant A → Plant B, **different GSTIN / state** | **Sale-purchase invoice** |
| An **accessory** used as raw material in the receiving plant | Delivery challan |
| **Finished goods** plant to plant | Sale-purchase invoice |
| **Recycling plant → any plant** | **Always** sale-purchase invoice — *"there is a recycling plant with a separate GST… whatever the recycling plant is producing is being used as input in other plants"* |

**The recycling plant is a separate GST entity.** Stated plainly, and consistent with the e-Way
Bill GSTIN analysis showing multiple registrations under one PAN.

**Concrete example (confirmed 2026-08-21):** **Units 6 (MS) and 7 (HDPE/IBC) share a GSTIN** —
both Bharuch. Movement between them uses a **delivery challan**. Unit 9 (recycling), also Bharuch,
holds a **separate** GSTIN — movement to/from Unit 9 always requires a sale-purchase invoice.

### Cross-state split fulfilment — an unsolved problem 🟡

> *"Cross-state purchase. Duplication issue… when half is coming from Gujarat, half is coming from
> Maharashtra."*

One customer order fulfilled from two plants in two states. The same-state portion attracts
**CGST+SGST**, the other **IGST**. Possibly two invoices from two entities.

> *"It's more a taxation question than a question of how it can be done on Phlo. So if we know the
> right rules and the right regulation, we'll be able to solve it."*

## 6. Job Work — Three Distinct Uses 🟡 (rec-32)

The ERP has two Labour Job Issue forms. Recording 32 describes **three** different uses:

| # | Use | Flow |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Machinery, equipment, tools and tooling** moved plant A → plant B | Job work challan |
| **B** | **Third party manufactures using Pyramid's own raw material** — *"in case of pallets, it's giving them the HDPE… the job work supplier will manufacture a pallet on behalf of Pyramid"* | Job work challan out; job worker raises a **service fee document** which Pyramid pays |
| **C** | **Internal, plant to plant** — *"in case there's a machinery breakdown or there's overcapacity in a singular plant, then that plant which is overloaded will give internal job work to another plant"* | Job work challan |

**Use C is significant and was entirely unknown.** Pyramid load-balances production between its own
plants using job work challans. That is a production-planning behaviour, not just a documentation
one, and it means a plant's output is not necessarily made at that plant.

## 7. Reuse, Refurbishment and Variable BOM 🟡 (rec-32, rec-33)

**Customers return packaging** 🟢 (both recordings): *"The IBC containers, cages, and pallets often
return back to Pyramid for reuse."* … *"Some customers also prefer a used cage and pallet. Meanwhile
we change the IBC which is inside."*

**The refurbishment problem:**

> *"Whenever Pyramid receives it, there are several possibilities where the pallet is damaged or the
> container is damaged, or the steel cage is damaged. And in that case, refurbishing that packaging
> material would require **variable BOM**, because we don't know which container or which IBC will be
> damaged by what part, and what part will be needed to make that IBC reusable again."*

- Refurbished containers are **sold to customers who accept them**, and *"a reused cage and reused pallet… might be cheaper"*
- *"You need to have all the spare parts available when managing refurbished packaging material"*

**Steel cannot be recycled, plastic can** 🟢 (rec-33): *"Steel, if not made correctly, gets wasted.
There's no recycling possible with steel. But with plastic it's possible — it can go back to the
recycling plant and [be] converted into granules and then eventually reusable once again."*

**Recycled granules are sold as well as reused** 🟢 (rec-32) — *"the recycled plastic granules are
sold as well as they are reused in the plant."* A revenue stream no document had recorded.

## 8. Rework on a Cancelled Sales Order 🟡 (rec-32)

> *"Let's say there's a standing sales order from **Grasim**, and for some reason Grasim cancels that
> order, and because it's a huge quantity, we need to make sure that the inventory leaves the
> facility because otherwise everything would come to a standstill."*

Pyramid **reassigns the order to a different party**, then physically alters the finished goods:

> *"Based on the different party's specification, they will modify the final finished goods in a
> manner to suit them. So in case there is a **valve change** or a possibility of **cage change** or
> **pallet change**… the material moves. There's a separate production process that happens which
> alters the final finished good."*

**Finished goods are not final.** They can be reworked into a different customer's specification —
which is the same base-product-plus-modification pattern seen in the screen printing and branded cap
seals, applied after the fact.

## Problems and Observations

| Observation | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Fleet **cost** is unmanaged and untracked | Half of "fleet management" was missing from every prior document |
| Cross-state split fulfilment is an **unsolved tax question** | Explicitly deferred as "a taxation question" — nobody owns it |
| Internal job work moves production between plants | A plant's output may not be made at that plant. Breaks naive per-plant capacity models |
| Refurbishment needs a **variable BOM** | Standard fixed-BOM inventory logic will not model it |
| Finished goods get **reworked** for a different customer | Stock is mutable after production |
| Material placement is informal | No bin/rack discipline to digitise — matches the blank Bin No. / Rack No. fields in the ERP |
| Steel scrap is a **loss**, plastic scrap is **recoverable** | Different economics, different handling, different accounting |
